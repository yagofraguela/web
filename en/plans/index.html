PHILOSOPHY AND APPROACH
How I work with you
TECHNIQUE COACHING

I analyse your videos to identify errors and areas to improve in your stroke. From there we work on each weakness with specific drills, rigging adjustments or simply cues to sharpen your technical awareness.

TRAINING PLANS

I follow the general lines of the training I have used in recent years to prepare for national selection trials and Spanish Championships, adding key details and adjustments from my experience as an elite athlete.

The process comes with constant feedback to adjust load, volume and sessions to your progress. We can also prepare specific erg tests and races, building a progression that brings you to peak form at the key moments of the season.

EVERY TRAINING PLAN IS FULLY INDIVIDUAL, ADAPTED TO EACH ATHLETE'S AVAILABILITY, GOALS AND FITNESS.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Everything you need to know before you start
Do I need to be a good rower to join?
Not at all. The main goal of the plans is each person's own progress. Every plan is fully adapted to the needs and level of each athlete.
How do I receive my training plan?
Once we are in touch, I'll send you your plans by email every month.
Are the plans only for racing?
Not necessarily. The idea is steady improvement and preparing each athlete's specific goals: performing better on the water, improving general fitness or building serious, effective training habits.
Does my level or age decide which plan I need?
No. All 3 plans adapt to any level and age. The choice depends only on how much support you want. To improve your technique on the water, choose technique coaching. If you only need training plans, there is a specific option. And for the most complete follow-up, combining technique and planning, PREMIUM is the one.
Can I change plan at any time?
Of course. The subscription is monthly, so you can change or cancel it at any time. Just email me and we'll sort it out.
Is there a trial, or can we talk first?
Email me and we can chat with no commitment. I'll answer any questions as soon as I can. Write to proyectocaetanohorta@gmail.com.
Can we work in English?
Yes. I can work with you in English or Spanish: plans, technical corrections and follow-up.

About me

FROM MOTOCROSS TO OLYMPIC ROWING

I was born in Noia, Galicia, and my first steps in sport were on a motorbike. I raced at regional and national level while taking my first strokes at Club do Mar Noia.

At 15 I joined the Galician High Performance Sports Centre (CGTD), where my story as a professional rower began.

Since then I have earned two Olympic diplomas, won the U23 World Championship in the lightweight single, a Junior European title and the Spanish lightweight erg record.

But the most important part is what comes next: sharing all of this with you.

Read my full story
THE ROOTS OF THE PROJECT

This project was born from the wish to give back to rowing everything the sport has given and taught me throughout my career. Every experience, every lesson and every training method I have lived through has shaped my own vision and way of working.

Today, all that knowledge comes together in a method built to help, guide and boost any rower who wants to keep improving and reach their best.
